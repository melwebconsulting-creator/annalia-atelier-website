The Artist & Philosophy

Thoughtful artistry focused on nail health and quiet luxury.

Founded by Annalia in Mississauga, Ontario, Annalia Atelier was built to redefine the salon experience into an intentional, unhurried 1-on-1 sanctuary.

Client Experience

Studio Policies & Etiquette

To ensure every client receives an unhurried, meticulous appointment, we kindly ask you to review our private studio standards.

Arrival & Bare Nails Etiquette

Please arrive with bare natural nails unless you booked a soak-off.
To preserve nail integrity and schedule accuracy, we do not soak off foreign MMA acrylic or dip powder applied elsewhere. If you have existing Gel-X done by Annalia Atelier, please select the removal option when scheduling.

Booking Deposit & Confirmation

A nominal deposit is required to reserve your private window.
Deposits are credited directly toward your total service fee on the day of your appointment. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit receipt is received.

24-Hour Rescheduling Notice

We respectfully request at least 24 hours notice for any changes.
As a private 1-on-1 studio, appointment slots are held exclusively for you. Rescheduling with 24h notice allows your deposit to roll over to a new date once.

Hygiene & Clinical Sterilization

Hospital-grade autoclaved implements and disposable files.
Your health and safety is paramount. All metal bits and cuticle tools undergo medical-grade disinfectant and sterilization between every client.
